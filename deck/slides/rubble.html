What’s the worth of ruling over nothing but rubble?

  • Palestinian steadfastness has always been about remaining on the land under overwhelming pressure
  • Gaza has been reduced to rubble, and the Palestinian national project remains on its deathbed, despite unprecedented popular support for its liberation movement
  • The international community will not finance the rebuilding of a territory governed by a people who are structurally committed to another catastrophe
  • The Virtuous City Vision affirms the Arab-Islamic nature of the Gaza Strip while engaging the West without capitulation or secular erasure

If Hamas seeks a future beyond governing ruins, endorsing the Virtuous City Vision is the most coherent path available to transform resistance into political survival, dignity, and renewal.
