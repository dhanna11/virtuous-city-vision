5 · Palestinian freedom of movement

A genuine choice for those who wish to leave

No people should be forcibly confined to a devastated enclave after mass destruction. Ending cycles of genocidal violence requires not only protection for those who remain, but a genuine choice for those who wish to leave.

  • The right to stay, leave, resettle, and return · guaranteed by the Coalition and binding international law
  • The Coalition will negotiate, coordinate, and ensure pathways for asylum, temporary evacuation, and voluntary relocation during the reconstruction period
  • Debated transparently and implemented through binding commitments by coalition member states · not left to ad hoc charity or discretionary border policies
