6 · Reconstruction · Palestinian labor

Gaza must be rebuilt by Palestinians themselves

  • A non-partisan Palestinian Labor Movement, fostered by the Board and Coalition, to absorb, preserve, and redeploy displaced Palestinian human capital
  • Centering Palestinian labor agency is the clearest check against reconstruction that erodes local ownership and political autonomy
  • Building on existing Palestinian labor institutions, outside factional politics during the transition
  • Workers, engineers, teachers, healthcare professionals, civil servants, and demobilized fighters who renounce armed violence · the Palestinian Authority’s reconstruction program as its baseline
