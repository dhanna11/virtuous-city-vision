The telos · one supreme purpose

True happiness (sa’ada)

To enable every inhabitant to attain true happiness: the full actualization of human potential through intellectual perfection, moral virtue, civic cooperation, and the ultimate union with the divine, culminating in eternal felicity.

  • A city is truly virtuous only when its leaders, institutions, education, and shared life mutually support this collective flourishing, in this world and the next
  • Each part of society functioning harmoniously, like organs in a healthy body

A nucleus for a new social order and contract among Gaza’s future inhabitants · the political and philosophical north star guiding reconstruction and nation-building after catastrophe
