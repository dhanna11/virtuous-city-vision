The West Bank · the test case

E1: where empty land must remain empty

The open land between Ma’ale Adumim and Jerusalem. Building it would, in AP’s words, “cut the West Bank in two”.

What Israel cites

  • State land, held by the Custodian of Government Property and marketed by the Israel Land Authority
  • Planning approval by the Civil Administration’s Higher Planning Council, under the Jordanian Planning Law of 1966 as amended by military order
  • A 1991 order placing part of E1 inside Ma’ale Adumim’s jurisdiction

Under the Holy Land Trust

  • E1 belongs in the empty-land account: the tenders are frozen
  • Israel gives a funded, easement-like undertaking not to build, without prejudice to title
  • If contracts are signed first, compensating the developers enters the account too
