E1: thirty years in the making

Jerusalem

E1 is the open land between Ma’ale Adumim and East Jerusalem. Built, it would cut East Jerusalem’s Palestinian neighborhoods off from the rest of the West Bank, and with them al-Quds, home of al-Aqsa and the Dome of the Rock.

The West Bank

It would split the northern West Bank from the southern, and critics say make a contiguous Palestinian state almost impossible. Israel says it serves security and does not block contiguity.

E1 is as much about the Islamic claim to Jerusalem as it is about the territorial integrity of the West Bank.
