Roughly 30 years: an incubation and three phases

0–2 years · Board of Peace incubation

  • Conclude the war via the Virtuous City Convention
  • Deploy the ISF security mandate
  • Fund and launch Gaza pilots
  • Activate NCAG governance
  • Execute initial DDR and disengagement
  • Formalize the Coalition for Canaan

0–10 years · coalition of the willing · stabilization

  • Full-scale demilitarization and infrastructure rebuild
  • Establish the transitional government
  • Support PA reforms toward statehood
  • Complete IDF disengagement from Gaza

10–20 years · alliance model · integration

  • Deepen Palestinian self-governance
  • Support for Palestinian state recognition
  • Abraham Accords expansion
  • Reconciliation and educational frameworks
  • Easing of movement restrictions
  • Initial Israeli disengagement from the West Bank

20–30 years · voluntary confederation · full maturity

  • Full mutual state recognition
  • Full integration of Israel, Palestine, and the wider Middle East
  • Full implementation of the reconciliation frameworks
  • Full freedom of movement
  • Final border demarcation and complete Israeli military withdrawal

Each phase after the incubation is an optional expanded mandate.
