The ISF

  • A UN and Board of Peace mandate
  • American-led, with an Indonesian deputy
  • Deploys and supports coalition-trained Palestinian forces in Gaza
  • A West Bank pilot

🤝

West Bank joint patrols

  • Integrated patrols with the IDF, the ISF, and Palestinian Authority forces

↩️

Israeli West Bank disengagement

  • Assisting the IDF and the PA during a phased Israeli withdrawal from the West Bank

Each step beyond the ISF is an optional expanded mandate.
